Home  /  Sunday School

Small classes.
Lasting foundations.

Our Sunday School gives children a positive place to learn about Islam, the Qur’an, and the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his pure family).

Learning with care

Room to learn at their own pace.

Our volunteer teachers support children as they build a strong foundation in Qur’an recitation, Arabic reading, and Islamic studies. Special attention is given to helping English-speaking students practice proper pronunciation step by step.

Classes are intentionally small — typically 6 to 18 students — so teachers can provide individual attention and encouragement.

Good to know

Questions from families.

How do I register my child?

Email jafferycenter@gmail.com to ask about registration, availability, and the current schedule.

How big are the classes?

Classes are typically 6 to 18 students, allowing volunteer teachers to give children individual attention.

Does my child need to know Arabic already?

Classes focus on helping English-speaking students build a foundation in Arabic reading and Qur’an pronunciation. Email the center to discuss your child’s needs.

How can we practice at home?

Regular short review sessions can help children build confidence over time. Ask the teachers what your child is currently learning so you can practice together.

Have a question about Sunday School?

We’d be happy to help you learn more about the program.
